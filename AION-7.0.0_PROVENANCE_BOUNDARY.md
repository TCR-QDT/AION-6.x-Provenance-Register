1. **Frozen historical record** — what 7.0.0 established, including the boundary.
2. **Authorization map** — which claims are permitted, which are forbidden, and by which invariant.

Any successor phase (7.1.x, 8.0.0, etc.) that operates within the Núcleo inherits these five properties.
